Considering that the subjects of the Postgraduate Entrance Preliminary Examination contain <b>Ideological and Political Theory</b>, <b>English Ⅰ</b>, and <b>Mathematics Ⅲ</b>, the study directions would be <b>(120100) Management Science & Engineering (MMgt)</b>[^1], <b>(120200) Business Administration (MMgt)</b>, or <b>(020200) Applied Economics (MEcon)</b>[^2]. Moreover, quantitative skills are crucial.
