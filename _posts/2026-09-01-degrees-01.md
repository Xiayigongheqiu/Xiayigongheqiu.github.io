Considering that the subjects of the Postgraduate Entrance Preliminary Examination contain <mark>English Ⅰ</mark> and <mark>Mathematics Ⅲ</mark>, the study directions probably can be <mark>(120100) Management Science & Engineering (MMgt)</mark>[^1], <mark>(020200) Applied Economics (MEcon)</mark>, or <mark>(120200) Business Administration (MMgt)</mark>[^2].<br>

In the future, the choices of study directions which shall be firstly considered are:
- (120100) Management Science and Engineering (MMgt)
- (020200) Applied Economics (MEcon)
- (120200) Business Administration (MMgt)

The locations of universities which shall be firstly considered are:
- Jinan
- <s>Shandong</s>
