Considering that the subjects of the Postgraduate Entrance Preliminary Examination contain <b>English Ⅰ</b> and <b>Mathematics Ⅲ</b>, the study directions probably can be <b>(120100) Management Science & Engineering (MMgt)</b>[^1], <b>(120200) Business Administration (MMgt)</b>, or <b>(020200) Applied Economics (MEcon)</b>[^2].<br>
Furthermore, theoretically, only those whose first four digits of the discipline codes are the same are not considered as cross-major in the Chinese postgraduate entrance examination (e.g., from 120203K to 120200).

In the future, the choices of study directions which shall be firstly considered are:
- (120100) Management Science and Engineering (MMgt)
- (120200) Business Administration (MMgt)
- (020200) Applied Economics (MEcon)

The locations of universities which shall be firstly considered are:
- Jinan
- <s>Shandong</s>
