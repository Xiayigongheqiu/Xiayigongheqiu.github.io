Considering that the subjects of the Postgraduate Entrance Preliminary Examination contain <strong>English Ⅰ</strong> and <strong>Mathematics Ⅲ</strong>, the study directions probably can be <strong>(120100) Management Science & Engineering (MMgt)</strong> or <strong>(020200) Applied Economics (MEcon)</strong>.<br>

In the future[^1], the choices of study directions which shall be firstly considered are:
- (120100) Management Science and Engineering (MMgt)
- (020200) Applied Economics (MEcon)
- (120210) Accounting (MMgt)

The locations of universities which shall be firstly considered are:
- Jinan
- Shandong
- North China
