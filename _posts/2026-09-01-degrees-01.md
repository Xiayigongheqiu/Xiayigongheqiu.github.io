Considering that the subjects of the Postgraduate Entrance Preliminary Examination contain <strong>English Ⅰ</strong> and <strong>Mathematics Ⅲ</strong>, the study directions probably can be <strong>(120100) Management Science & Engineering (MMgt)</strong>[^1] or <strong>(120200) Business Administration (MMgt)</strong>[^2].<br>

In the future, the choices of study directions which shall be firstly considered are:
- (120100) Management Science and Engineering (MMgt)
- (120200) Business Administration (MMgt)

The locations of universities which shall be firstly considered are:
- Jinan
- <s>Shandong</s>
