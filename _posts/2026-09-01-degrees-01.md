Considering that the subjects of the Postgraduate Entrance Preliminary Examination contain Ideological and Political Theory, English Ⅰ, Mathematics Ⅲ, and Management, the study directions could only be (120100) Management Science and Engineering[^1] or (120200) Business Administration[^2]. The target university could only be the [University of Jinan](https://www.ujn.edu.cn/). Both the study directions are possible, so the current study emphasis is English and mathematics.
