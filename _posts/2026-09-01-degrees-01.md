Considering that the subjects of the Postgraduate Entrance Preliminary Examination contain <strong>English Ⅰ</strong> and <strong>Mathematics Ⅲ</strong>, the study directions probably can be <strong>(120100) Management Science & Engineering (MMgt)</strong>[^1] <s>or (020200) Applied Economics (MEcon)</s>.<br>

In the future, the choices of study directions which shall be firstly considered are:
- <strong>(120100) Management Science and Engineering (MMgt)</strong>
- <s>(020200) Applied Economics (MEcon)</s>

The locations of universities which shall be firstly considered are:
- <strong>Jinan</strong>
- <s>Shandong</s>
- <s>North China</s>
