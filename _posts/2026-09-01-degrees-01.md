Considering that the subjects of the Postgraduate Entrance Preliminary Examination contain <strong>English Ⅰ</strong> and <strong>Mathematics Ⅲ</strong>, the study directions probably can be <strong>(120100) Management Science & Engineering (MMgt)</strong> or (020200) Applied Economics (MEcon).<br>

In the future, the choices of study directions which shall be firstly considered are:[^1]
- <strong>(120100) Management Science and Engineering (MMgt)</strong>[^2]
- (020200) Applied Economics (MEcon)

The locations of universities which shall be firstly considered are:[^1]
- <strong>Jinan</strong>[^2]
- Shandong
- North China
