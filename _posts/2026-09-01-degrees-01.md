Considering that the subjects of the Postgraduate Entrance Preliminary Examination contain <b>English Ⅰ</b> and <b>Mathematics Ⅲ</b>, the study directions would be <b>(120100) Management Science & Engineering (MMgt)</b>[^1], <b>(120200) Business Administration (MMgt)</b>, or <b>(020200) Applied Economics (MEcon)</b>[^2]. And the target university would be the [University of Jinan](https://www.ujn.edu.cn/). All the three study directions are possible[^3], so the current study emphasis is English and mathematics.
