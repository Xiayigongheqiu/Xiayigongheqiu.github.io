## <ruby>Sum-of-Squares Zero Matrix Criterion<rt>平方和零矩阵判定准则</rt></ruby>
For a real matrix *A* = (*a<sub>ij</sub>*), if
```math
\sum_{i=1}^{m} \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{ij}^{2}=0
```
then *A* is the <ruby>zero matrix<rt>*O*</rt></ruby>.
