About two years ago, I was interested in <ruby>Chinese characters<rt>CJKV</rt></ruby> and <ruby>Kanbun<rt>漢文</rt></ruby>. In those days, I found that some of the loanwords which are written in Katakana in Japanese can be transliterated into Kanji gracefully (i.e. "<ruby>ateji<rt>当て字</rt></ruby>"). I came up with some good examples, but now I can recollect few of them. Here are a few.

|Words in the source language|Katakana|Ateji|
|---|---|---|
|dream|ドリーム|渡璃夢|
|cold|コルド|湖流凍|

And here are other examples: [https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Lh4y1q7zJ/](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Lh4y1q7zJ/)<br>
I find it interesting and beautiful so I record it here.
