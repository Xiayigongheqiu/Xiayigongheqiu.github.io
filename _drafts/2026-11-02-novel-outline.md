# A Novel Outline

> This novel outline will be based on my fantasies, especially my past dreams, e.g., being a woman like Yamato Nadeshiko. Although I have been fulfilling some of my dreams, some things are fated to be impossible in this world; therefore, I decide to achieve some of what is impossible here in a more fantastical way.<br>
> AI will be used for the novel.

([Preliminary Reference Settings](https://xiayigongheqiu.github.io/2026/10/29/OC.html))

---

The protagonist is named Shrimpball (or Xiāqiú in Chinese), who has been transfeminine since adolescence. *She* dreamed of being a good girl, a good wife, and even a good mother, all of which were impossible in the real world. Nonetheless, she kept dreaming. During her time at a troubled high school and in the period after graduation, her physical and mental health was getting worse, so was her financial situation; as a result, she faced the kinds of hardships that transgender people commonly face. During this difficult period, she was transported to a fantasy world and lived out her life there. Finally, she died in the fantasy world and woke up in the real world as if from a long dream.

1. Emphasize the C-PTSD (especially cognitive distortions) caused by her experiences during her troubled high school years.
2. Her first attempt to build a life ended after a desperate act of self-harm (possibly an overdose), after which she was transported to a fantasy world.
3. The new world gave her new hope. She began to save herself by studying mathematics and working as a maid. In addition, her assigned sex was altered.
4. At an important event, she was hired by a vampire noble owning a grand mansion or estate. She entered noble social circles.
5. Years passed; she rose from maid to the master's wife, and finally became a mother.
6. Finally she passed away and woke up in the real world.

## Summary of Suggestions for the Novel Outline

### 1. Theme and Character
- Shrimpball should first be a full character, not merely a transgender character; avoid letting “issues” swallow “personhood.”
- Ask: once she actually becomes a “good wife” or “good mother” in the other world, does she still want those identities?
- Connect her mathematical ability to her identity exploration—for example, is the world of “proof and certainty” a way of escaping the “unsolvable” real world?

### 2. Outline Structure: From Event List to Causal Chain
- The current bullet points are mostly parallel and lack causal momentum.
- Try rewriting them as a “because A, therefore B, but C” chain:
  - Because her first attempt at living in reality fails, she enters the other world with unresolved C-PTSD;
  - But the new hope in the other world does not automatically heal trauma; cognitive distortions recur in new forms;
  - Therefore, when she becomes a wife and mother, the core conflict is: is she living out her dream, or performing a newly assigned role?
- Make “self-harm → isekai arrival” thematically resonant: what was she trying to end, or escape from?

### 3. Presenting C-PTSD
- Do not merely “emphasize” symptoms; make cognitive distortions the narrative engine.
- For example, a core distortion such as “I don’t deserve good things” or “any improvement is temporary” can actively create conflict, self-sabotage, and misinterpretation of kindness.
- Let trauma affect emotional regulation and self-perception, so the narrative reflects a non-linear mind shaped by trauma.

### 4. GitHub and Markdown Suggestions
- The current single file is too dense; split it by dimension:
  - `characters.md`: inner conflicts, desires, fears, false beliefs;
  - `theme.md`: one sentence stating what the story is arguing about;
  - `timeline.md`: time scales, ages, and durations in reality and the other world.
- Write specific commit messages, e.g. `outline: add causal chain from self-harm to isekai arrival`.

### 5. AI-Assistance Reminder
- AI is good at expansion and stylization, not at making causal decisions for you.
- Write `characters.md` and `theme.md` clearly before letting AI expand chapters.
- Avoid writing polished chapters before the core conflict is settled; otherwise revision costs will be high.

### 6. One Sentence
- Turn Shrimpball from “a transgender person who experienced trauma” into a complete person who makes surprising choices.
- Of everything she gains in the other world, which parts does she truly want, and which parts does she only think she wants—the answer is the novel.
